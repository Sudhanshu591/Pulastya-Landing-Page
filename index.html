How Pulastya works

From the first ring to the call record

How a call moves through the components you configure in Pulastya.

  1. 01

    Call connects

    An inbound or outbound call runs through your Twilio or Exotel number.

  2. 02

    Speech is processed

    Caller audio is transcribed live. Real-time OpenAI voice streaming currently runs on Twilio.

  3. 03

    Intent and context

    Your active prompt template sets the agent's role, domain and behaviour for the call.

  4. 04

    Approved knowledge

    The agent can cite the PDF documents you have uploaded to Knowledge.

  5. 05

    Response or handoff

    The agent replies in the voice you selected, or the call is transferred to your team.

  6. 06

    Call record

    Status, transcript, summary, intent and sentiment are saved to call history.

Full overview of Pulastya

One view of the Pulastya workspace, with the parts covered in the tabs below: voice and routing, knowledge, prompt templates, calls and handoff.

Choose the voice and the telephony provider

Configure the voice your agent uses on calls and connect the telephony provider that carries them.

  • Voice providers: Twilio Polly (Amazon Polly or Google TTS), OpenAI TTS and ElevenLabs
  • Indian, British and US English voices, male and female
  • Twilio is the default telephony provider; Exotel is available for basic call flows
  • Use your own caller number from Twilio or Exotel

Ground answers in your approved documents

Upload PDFs the agent can cite during calls, so responses stay within the business information you provide.

  • Upload PDF documents to Knowledge
  • Search documents and preview their content section by section
  • Reprocess a knowledge source after you update it

Define how the agent behaves on every call

Prompt templates hold the system prompt that is sent to the AI on every call.

  • Organise templates by domain: Generic, Healthcare, E-commerce, Banking, Real Estate, Hospitality and Education
  • Start from a domain starter prompt, then edit it for your business
  • Set the template your agent should use as active

Monitor live calls and review call history

The dashboard shows every call with its status, so teams can see what is live, completed or transferred.

  • Totals for calls, completed calls, transferred calls and average duration
  • Filter by Live, Transferred and Completed, and by Today, 7 days or All time
  • Search by phone number and export the call list

Read the transcript and the context of each call

Open a call to review what the caller and the agent said, turn by turn.

  • Transcript with speaker labels and timestamps
  • Call records include a summary, intent and sentiment
  • Admins can inspect calls across users at transcript level, including call SID, provider and status

Run a test call from your browser

Check how the agent sounds and responds before it handles real callers.

  • Browser test call with your live microphone, AI audio and a live transcript
  • Test AI Chat for checking responses in text
  • Test calls appear in call history alongside other calls
Live Transferred to your team Completed by the AI agent

Hand conversations to your team when needed

Calls that move from the AI agent to a person are recorded as transferred, so your team can follow up with context.

  • Transferred calls have their own status, filter and total on the dashboard
  • An agent phone number is part of the account's call setup
  • Transfer is supported on Exotel call flows as well as Twilio
  • Transfer and escalation events can be mapped to your systems through the Pulastya API

Security and governance

Controls built into the workspace

How Pulastya handles credentials, access and oversight.

Encrypted BYOK credentials

Telephony and OpenAI credentials are saved encrypted for your account, and saved values are masked in the interface.

Credential verification

Pulastya verifies Twilio and OpenAI credentials, and the Calling Console stays locked until credentials are saved.

Role-based access

Access is assigned by role: user, manager, admin and super admin. Only super admins can change roles.

Activity and audit view

Admins get a combined view of audit logs, calls, payments, notifications and document uploads.
